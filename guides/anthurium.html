Anthurium care guide

Anthurium andraeanum — Waxy heart blooms; warmth and humidity help.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 5 days ☀️ Partial Shade
WaterEvery 5 days
LightPartial Shade
Humidity60%+
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Anthurium

Slightly moist, never soggy.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 5 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright indirect.

Soil & fertilizer

Chunky aroid blend.

Bloom feed biweekly.

Common problems

  • Brown tips
  • No flowers

Pro tips

  • Wipe spathes
  • Keep above 16°C

FAQ

How often should I water Anthurium?

Slightly moist, never soggy.

Is Anthurium safe for pets?

Anthurium is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Bright indirect.
