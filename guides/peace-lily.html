Peace Lily care guide

Spathiphyllum wallisii — Elegant blooms; droops clearly when thirsty.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 4 days ☀️ Partial Shade
WaterEvery 4 days
LightPartial Shade
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Peace Lily

Evenly moist; recovers after droop.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 4 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Medium to low indirect.

Soil & fertilizer

Peat-rich mix.

Half-strength monthly.

Common problems

  • Brown tips
  • No blooms

Pro tips

  • Mist for humidity
  • Toxic to pets

FAQ

How often should I water Peace Lily?

Evenly moist; recovers after droop.

Is Peace Lily safe for pets?

Peace Lily is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Medium to low indirect.
