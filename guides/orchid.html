Phalaenopsis Orchid care guide

Phalaenopsis spp. — Long-blooming moth orchid for bright spots.

🐾 Pet safer 💧 Every 7 days ☀️ Partial Shade
WaterEvery 7 days
LightPartial Shade
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Phalaenopsis Orchid

Weekly soak; drain fully.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 7 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright east window.

Soil & fertilizer

Orchid bark only.

Weakly weekly.

Common problems

  • Crown rot
  • Wrinkled leaves

Pro tips

  • Room-temp water
  • Keep spike after bloom

FAQ

How often should I water Phalaenopsis Orchid?

Weekly soak; drain fully.

Is Phalaenopsis Orchid safe for pets?

Phalaenopsis Orchid is generally considered a pet-safer choice. Still supervise curious pets.

What light does it need?

Bright east window.
