Bonsai Pine care guide

Pinus thunbergii — Traditional pine bonsai needing full sun and attentive water.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 2 days ☀️ Full Sun
WaterEvery 2 days
LightFull Sun
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Bonsai Pine

Check daily in summer.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 2 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Full sun outdoors.

Soil & fertilizer

Inorganic bonsai mix.

Bonsai feed in growth.

Common problems

  • Needle issues
  • Underwatering

Pro tips

  • Outdoor culture
  • Wire carefully

FAQ

How often should I water Bonsai Pine?

Check daily in summer.

Is Bonsai Pine safe for pets?

Bonsai Pine is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Full sun outdoors.
