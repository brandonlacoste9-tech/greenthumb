Tradescantia Zebrina care guide

LightPartial Sun

Watering Tradescantia Zebrina

Slightly moist.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 5 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

How often should I water Tradescantia Zebrina?

Slightly moist.

Is Tradescantia Zebrina safe for pets?

Tradescantia Zebrina is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.
