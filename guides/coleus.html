Coleus care guide

Plectranthus scutellarioides — Painted foliage color bomb for pots and beds.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 3 days ☀️ Partial Sun
WaterEvery 3 days
LightPartial Sun
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Coleus

Even moisture.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 3 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright; some sun for color.

Soil & fertilizer

Rich mix.

Regular light feed.

Common problems

  • Legginess
  • Heat wilt

Pro tips

  • Pinch flowers
  • Easy cuttings

FAQ

How often should I water Coleus?

Even moisture.

Is Coleus safe for pets?

Coleus is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Bright; some sun for color.
