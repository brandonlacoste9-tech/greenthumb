Japanese Maple care guide

Acer palmatum — Elegant outdoor tree with seasonal color.

🐾 Pet safer 💧 Every 3 days ☀️ Partial Sun

Watering Japanese Maple

Even moisture in pots.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 3 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Morning sun, afternoon shade.

Soil & fertilizer

Acidic well-draining loam.

Slow-release spring.

Common problems

  • Leaf scorch
  • Drought stress

Pro tips

  • Mulch roots
  • Protect late frost

FAQ

How often should I water Japanese Maple?

Even moisture in pots.

Is Japanese Maple safe for pets?

Japanese Maple is generally considered a pet-safer choice. Still supervise curious pets.

What light does it need?

Morning sun, afternoon shade.
