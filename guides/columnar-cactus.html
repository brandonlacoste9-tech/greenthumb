Watering Columnar Cactus

Rare deep soaks; dry winter.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 21 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Full sun.

FAQ

How often should I water Columnar Cactus?

Rare deep soaks; dry winter.

Is Columnar Cactus safe for pets?

Columnar Cactus is generally considered a pet-safer choice. Still supervise curious pets.

What light does it need?

Full sun.
