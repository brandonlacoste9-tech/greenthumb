Chinese Evergreen care guide

Aglaonema spp. — Colorful low-light champion for offices.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 9 days ☀️ Full Shade
WaterEvery 9 days
LightFull Shade
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Chinese Evergreen

Moderate dry-down.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 9 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Low to medium.

Soil & fertilizer

Well-draining mix.

Light monthly.

Common problems

  • Cold damage
  • Overwatering

Pro tips

  • Avoid drafts
  • Many cultivars

FAQ

How often should I water Chinese Evergreen?

Moderate dry-down.

Is Chinese Evergreen safe for pets?

Chinese Evergreen is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Low to medium.
