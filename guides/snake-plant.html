Snake Plant care guide

Dracaena trifasciata — Nearly indestructible upright plant that forgives missed waterings.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 14 days ☀️ Full Sun
WaterEvery 14 days
LightFull Sun
HumidityLow–medium
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Snake Plant

Every 2–3 weeks when fully dry.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 14 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Low to bright light.

Soil & fertilizer

Cactus/succulent mix.

2–3× per year.

Common problems

  • Mushy base
  • Wrinkling

Pro tips

  • Bedroom friendly
  • Leaf cuttings propagate

FAQ

How often should I water Snake Plant?

Every 2–3 weeks when fully dry.

Is Snake Plant safe for pets?

Snake Plant is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Low to bright light.
