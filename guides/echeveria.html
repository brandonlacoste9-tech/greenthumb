Echeveria care guide

Echeveria spp. — Rosette succulents that color up in strong light.

🐾 Pet safer 💧 Every 12 days ☀️ Full Sun
WaterEvery 12 days
LightFull Sun
HumidityLow–medium
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Echeveria

Bottom water when dry.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 12 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Full sun / very bright.

Soil & fertilizer

Gritty cactus mix.

Rare spring.

Common problems

  • Etiolation
  • Mealybugs
  • Rot

Pro tips

  • Avoid wet rosette
  • Summer outdoors

FAQ

How often should I water Echeveria?

Bottom water when dry.

Is Echeveria safe for pets?

Echeveria is generally considered a pet-safer choice. Still supervise curious pets.

What light does it need?

Full sun / very bright.
