Lucky Bamboo care guide

Dracaena sanderiana — Often grown in water; actually a dracaena.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 7 days ☀️ Partial Shade
WaterEvery 7 days
LightPartial Shade
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Lucky Bamboo

Change vase water weekly.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 7 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright indirect.

Soil & fertilizer

Water culture or moist soil.

Tiny monthly dose.

Common problems

  • Algae
  • Chlorine yellowing

Pro tips

  • Filtered water
  • Not true bamboo

FAQ

How often should I water Lucky Bamboo?

Change vase water weekly.

Is Lucky Bamboo safe for pets?

Lucky Bamboo is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Bright indirect.
