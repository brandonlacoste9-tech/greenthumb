Fiddle Leaf Fig care guide

Ficus lyrata — Statement tree that wants stable light and watering.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 7 days ☀️ Full Sun
WaterEvery 7 days
LightFull Sun
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Fiddle Leaf Fig

When top 5 cm dry; soak thoroughly.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 7 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright consistent window light.

Soil & fertilizer

Well-draining indoor mix.

Biweekly in season.

Common problems

  • Brown spots
  • Leaf drop after moves

Pro tips

  • Rotate monthly
  • Avoid cold drafts

FAQ

How often should I water Fiddle Leaf Fig?

When top 5 cm dry; soak thoroughly.

Is Fiddle Leaf Fig safe for pets?

Fiddle Leaf Fig is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Bright consistent window light.
