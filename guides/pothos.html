Pothos care guide

Epipremnum aureum — Trailing workhorse for almost any indoor spot.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 7 days ☀️ Partial Shade
WaterEvery 7 days
LightPartial Shade
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Pothos

When top half of soil is dry.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 7 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Low to bright indirect.

Soil & fertilizer

Standard potting mix.

Monthly spring–fall.

Common problems

  • Yellow leaves
  • Leggy growth

Pro tips

  • Trim to bush out
  • Easy water prop

FAQ

How often should I water Pothos?

When top half of soil is dry.

Is Pothos safe for pets?

Pothos is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Low to bright indirect.
