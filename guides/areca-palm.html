Areca Palm care guide

Dypsis lutescens — Pet-safe tropical palm for bright rooms.

🐾 Pet safer 💧 Every 5 days ☀️ Partial Sun
WaterEvery 5 days

Watering Areca Palm

Lightly moist; reduce winter.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 5 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright indirect.

Soil & fertilizer

Palm mix.

Palm feed monthly.

Common problems

  • Spider mites
  • Tip burn

Pro tips

  • Flush soil
  • Pet safe

FAQ

How often should I water Areca Palm?

Lightly moist; reduce winter.

Is Areca Palm safe for pets?

Areca Palm is generally considered a pet-safer choice. Still supervise curious pets.

What light does it need?

Bright indirect.
