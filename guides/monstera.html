Monstera Deliciosa care guide

Monstera deliciosa — Fenestrated climber for bright rooms with a moss pole.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 7 days ☀️ Partial Sun
WaterEvery 7 days
LightPartial Sun
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Monstera Deliciosa

Weekly; let top third dry.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 7 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright indirect for fenestrations.

Soil & fertilizer

Chunky aroid mix.

Monthly in season.

Common problems

  • Yellow leaves
  • Crispy edges

Pro tips

  • Use a moss pole
  • Wipe leaves monthly

FAQ

How often should I water Monstera Deliciosa?

Weekly; let top third dry.

Is Monstera Deliciosa safe for pets?

Monstera Deliciosa is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Bright indirect for fenestrations.
