Geranium care guide

Pelargonium × hortorum — Sunny patio bloomer; slight dry-down between waterings.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 4 days ☀️ Full Sun
WaterEvery 4 days
LightFull Sun
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Geranium

Moderate; avoid constant wet.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 4 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Full sun.

Soil & fertilizer

Well-draining mix.

Bloom feed biweekly.

Common problems

  • Edema
  • Botrytis

Pro tips

  • Deadhead
  • Overwinter bright

FAQ

How often should I water Geranium?

Moderate; avoid constant wet.

Is Geranium safe for pets?

Geranium is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Full sun.
