Haworthia care guide

Haworthia spp. — Zebra succulent for windowsills with softer light needs.

🐾 Pet safer 💧 Every 16 days ☀️ Partial Sun
WaterEvery 16 days
LightPartial Sun
HumidityLow–medium
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Haworthia

Sparse; dry completely.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 16 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright indirect.

Soil & fertilizer

Gritty mix.

Almost never.

Common problems

  • Translucent leaves
  • Stretching

Pro tips

  • Shallow pots
  • Pet safer

FAQ

How often should I water Haworthia?

Sparse; dry completely.

Is Haworthia safe for pets?

Haworthia is generally considered a pet-safer choice. Still supervise curious pets.

What light does it need?

Bright indirect.
