String of Hearts care guide

Ceropegia woodii — Trailing hearts with tubers; bright light for color.

🐾 Pet safer 💧 Every 12 days ☀️ Partial Sun

Watering String of Hearts

Sparse; tubers store water.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 12 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright + some sun.

Soil & fertilizer

Rare.

How often should I water String of Hearts?
