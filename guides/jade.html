Jade Plant care guide

Crassula ovata — Woody succulent shrub with plump leaves.

⚠️ Toxic to pets 💧 Every 14 days ☀️ Full Sun
WaterEvery 14 days
LightFull Sun
HumidityLow–medium
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Jade Plant

Soak and dry fully.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 14 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright / some direct sun.

Soil & fertilizer

Cactus mix.

Dilute spring only.

Common problems

  • Leaf drop
  • Etiolation

Pro tips

  • Prune tree form
  • Long-lived

FAQ

How often should I water Jade Plant?

Soak and dry fully.

Is Jade Plant safe for pets?

Jade Plant is considered toxic to pets. Keep it out of reach of cats and dogs.

What light does it need?

Bright / some direct sun.
