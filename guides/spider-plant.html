Spider Plant care guide

Chlorophytum comosum — Air-purifying classic that thrives with bright indirect light.

🐾 Pet safer 💧 Every 7 days ☀️ Partial Sun
WaterEvery 7 days
LightPartial Sun
Humidity40–60%
Temperature15–27°C

Watering Spider Plant

Water when top 2–3 cm is dry.

As a baseline, plan on watering about every 7 days, then adjust for pot size, light, and season. In winter, many plants need less water.

Light requirements

Bright indirect; avoid harsh midday sun.

Soil & fertilizer

Loose indoor mix.

Monthly in growing season.

Common problems

  • Brown tips
  • Root rot

Pro tips

  • Propagate plantlets
  • Great in hanging baskets

FAQ

How often should I water Spider Plant?

Water when top 2–3 cm is dry.

Is Spider Plant safe for pets?

Spider Plant is generally considered a pet-safer choice. Still supervise curious pets.

What light does it need?

Bright indirect; avoid harsh midday sun.
