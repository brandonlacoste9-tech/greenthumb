About GardenGreen

GardenGreen helps you identify plants, schedule watering with local weather in mind, and keep a simple care history.

Built for people who want clarity — not another noisy app. Most data stays on your device unless you enable cloud sync.

Live at www.garden-green.co.

Privacy

1. Your data

We don’t sell plant photos or care data.

2. Local first

Garden and ledger data live in your browser storage by default.

3. AI

Optional cloud ID uses your photo only for that request. We don’t train general models on your personal library.
